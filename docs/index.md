## About Me

I'm Bryan Tan, a compiler engineer with a love for programming language theory.
I currently work at [Veridise](https://veridise.com), where I develop static
analysis and formal verification tools that grew out of my [past
research](research.md) and perform secure code review for clients.
Previously, I worked at AWS Annapurna Labs on the AWS Neuron compiler frontend
for the Inferentia and Tranium AI accelerators.
I have a variety of interests, but I am most excited about static analysis,
compiler optimizations, and domain-specific languages.
Check out my [blog](blog/index.md) or my [academic research profile page](research.md) to
get a glimpse of what I like to do!

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/Technius) and
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bryan-tan-08153079). I'm also reachable
by email at `bryantan (AT) technius (DOT) net`.
